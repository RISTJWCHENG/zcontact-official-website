Why zContact

Private by design, not by promise.

zContact is built so that nobody in the middle — including us — can read what you send.

End-to-end encrypted

Messages, voice messages, calls and files are encrypted on your device with the MLS protocol. We cannot read their content.

No passwords

Sign in with a passkey, Internet Identity or a code sent to your email. There is no password to steal or forget.

Direct when online

When the person you write to is online, messages and calls go straight between your devices where possible.

Encrypted mailbox

Offline? Your encrypted message waits in a mailbox on the Internet Computer and is deleted once delivered, or after 30 days at most.

Voice & video calls

Call your contacts from the chat, encrypted end to end, with voice messages for when a call doesn't fit.

Your own storage

Optionally connect your own Sia storage for backups and large files. Everything is encrypted on your device before it is stored.

How it works

Only the people in the conversation can read it.

From the moment you press send to the moment it arrives, your message stays encrypted.

01

Encrypted on your device

Your message is encrypted with keys that are created and kept on your device, before it leaves your phone or browser.

02

Sent directly, or held safely

It goes straight to your contact's device when they are online, or waits encrypted in an Internet Computer mailbox when they are not.

03

Decrypted only by them

Only your contact's devices can decrypt it. The mailbox copy is removed as soon as it is delivered.
